**AWS Support plans** decide how fast and how much technical help you get. AWS **restructured** the plans on **2 December 2025**, but many practice exams (and possibly the live exam pool) still use the **classic** plans. Learn both.

## The current plans

| Plan | Key points |
|---|---|
| **Basic** (free, every account) | 24/7 customer service for **billing and account** questions, documentation, whitepapers, **AWS re:Post**, the Health Dashboard, **core** Trusted Advisor checks. **No technical support cases** |
| **Business Support+** (from about $29/month) | **Replaces Developer and Business.** AI-assisted troubleshooting plus 24/7 access to AWS experts; unlimited users can open technical cases |
| **Enterprise Support** (from about $5,000/month) | For **business-critical** workloads: a designated **Technical Account Manager (TAM)**, proactive guidance |
| **Unified Operations** | The top tier, for mission-critical, large-scale operations |

## The classic (legacy) plans

No longer sold to new customers since December 2025, and discontinued on 1 January 2027, but still common in practice questions:

| Plan | Fastest response | Notable features |
|---|---|---|
| **Developer** | < 12 business hours (system impaired) | Business-hours email access to cloud support associates; for testing and early development |
| **Business** | < **1 hour** (production system down) | **24/7 phone, chat and email**; **full Trusted Advisor** checks; AWS Support API |
| **Enterprise On-Ramp** | < **30 minutes** (business-critical system down) | A **pool** of TAMs; concierge support |
| **Enterprise** | < **15 minutes** (business-critical system down) | A **designated TAM**, concierge billing team, Infrastructure Event Management, Well-Architected reviews |

## Exam rules of thumb

| The question says… | Answer |
|---|---|
| "**Technical Account Manager**" (designated) | **Enterprise** |
| "**15-minute** response for business-critical systems" | **Enterprise** |
| "full Trusted Advisor checks + 24/7 phone support at the **lowest cost**" | **Business** (now **Business Support+**) |
| "only **billing and account** questions" | **Basic** |
| "experimenting / testing", "business-hours email" | **Developer** (legacy) |

## Check yourself

1. Which plan includes a designated Technical Account Manager?
2. A company needs 24/7 phone support and all Trusted Advisor checks at the lowest cost. Which plan?
3. What does the free Basic plan **not** include?

### Answers

1. Enterprise Support.
2. Business (now Business Support+).
3. Technical support cases (it covers only billing and account questions, plus docs, re:Post and core Trusted Advisor checks).
