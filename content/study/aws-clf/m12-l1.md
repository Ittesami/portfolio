The **AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (AWS CAF)** is AWS's guidance for planning a **successful move to the cloud**. Its main idea: a migration isn't just a technical project. Business, people and processes have to change too.

## The six perspectives

CAF groups the capabilities an organisation needs into **six perspectives**:

| Perspective | Focus | Typical stakeholders |
|---|---|---|
| **Business** | Cloud investments **accelerate business outcomes**; align IT with business strategy | CEO, CFO, COO, CIO, CTO |
| **People** | **Culture**, skills, **training**, organisational change, staffing | HR, staffing, people managers |
| **Governance** | Orchestrate cloud initiatives, maximise benefits, **minimise risk**; manage and measure investments | CIO, programme managers, enterprise architects, business analysts |
| **Platform** | Build a **scalable, hybrid cloud platform**; architecture, modernise workloads | CTO, IT managers, solutions architects |
| **Security** | **Confidentiality, integrity and availability** of data and workloads | CISO, IT security managers and analysts |
| **Operations** | Cloud services delivered at the **agreed level**; monitoring, incident management | IT operations and support managers |

### Business vs technical

- **Business, People, Governance** = the **business** capabilities.
- **Platform, Security, Operations** = the **technical** capabilities.

## How to answer CAF questions

Match the **concern** or the **job title** to the perspective:

| The question mentions… | Perspective |
|---|---|
| Training staff, skills gaps, culture, HR | **People** |
| Business outcomes, strategy, the CFO's investment case | **Business** |
| Risk, measuring benefits, programme management | **Governance** |
| Architecture, building the cloud environment | **Platform** |
| Protecting data, the CISO | **Security** |
| Monitoring, incidents, service levels | **Operations** |

## Also good to know

- CAF transformation **domains**: technology, process, organisation, product.
- CAF **phases**: **envision → align → launch → scale**.
