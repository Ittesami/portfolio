The **AWS Free Tier** lets new customers try AWS without paying. It **changed in July 2025**, and practice exams may ask about **either** version, so know both.

## The new model (accounts created on or after 15 July 2025)

- At sign-up you choose a **Free plan** or a **Paid plan**.
- You get **$100 in credits**, plus **up to $100 more** for completing onboarding activities (for example, setting up an AWS Budget).
- The **Free plan** lasts **6 months or until your credits run out**, whichever comes first.
- On the Free plan you are **never charged**. When it ends, the account **closes unless you upgrade** to a Paid plan.

## Always Free (still there)

Some offers never expire, for every account. For example:

- **AWS Lambda:** 1 million requests per month.
- **Amazon DynamoDB:** 25 GB of storage.

## The legacy model (older accounts and older question banks)

Three types of offer:

| Type | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Always Free** | Never expires | Lambda 1 million requests/month |
| **12 Months Free** | Free for 12 months after sign-up | **750 hours/month** of t2/t3.micro EC2 |
| **Trials** | Short-term free trials of specific services | A service free for 30 days |

If a practice question asks about "three types of Free Tier offers", it's using this legacy model.

## Staying safe on the Free Tier

- Set an **AWS Budget** with an alert (it also earns onboarding credits under the new model).
- Delete resources you no longer need (EC2 instances, NAT gateways and unattached volumes cost money outside the free amounts).

## Check yourself

1. Under the new model, how long does the Free plan last?
2. What happens when a Free-plan account's credits or time run out?
3. Name the three legacy Free Tier offer types.

### Answers

1. 6 months or until the credits are used up, whichever comes first.
2. The account closes unless you upgrade to a Paid plan; you're never charged on the Free plan.
3. Always Free, 12 Months Free, and Trials.
