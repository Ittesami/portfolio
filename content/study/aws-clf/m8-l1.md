AWS offers AI and machine learning at **three layers**, depending on how much ML expertise and control you want. Most exam questions here are "which AI service does this task?", so learn the one-liners.

## Layer 1: build your own models: Amazon SageMaker AI

For data scientists and ML engineers who want to **build, train and deploy their own models** at scale: notebooks, training jobs, model hosting, and **SageMaker Canvas** for no-code ML.

Keyword: "**build, train and deploy** custom machine-learning models".

## Layer 2: generative AI

- **Amazon Bedrock:** a fully managed service for building **generative AI** apps with **foundation models** (Anthropic Claude, Amazon Nova, Meta Llama, Mistral and more) through **one API**, with no infrastructure to manage. Supports **knowledge bases** (RAG: answering from your own documents), **agents** and **guardrails**.
- **Amazon Q:** AWS's generative-AI **assistant**:
  - **Q Business** answers questions over your **company's data**.
  - **Q Developer** helps with **coding** and with AWS in the console.

Keyword: "**foundation models**", "generative AI", "build a chatbot with an LLM" → **Bedrock**.

## Layer 3: pre-trained AI services (no ML expertise needed)

Just call an API.

| Service | What it does | Memory hook |
|---|---|---|
| **Amazon Rekognition** | Image and video analysis: faces, objects, text, unsafe content | "**Recognise**" pictures |
| **Amazon Textract** | Extract text, forms and tables from **scanned documents** | "**Text extract**" |
| **Amazon Comprehend** | Natural-language processing: **sentiment**, key phrases, entities, PII in text | "**Comprehend**" the meaning |
| **Amazon Transcribe** | **Speech → text** | Transcription |
| **Amazon Polly** | **Text → speech** (lifelike voices) | Polly the parrot **talks** |
| **Amazon Translate** | Translate between languages | Translate |
| **Amazon Lex** | Build **chatbots** and voice bots (the technology behind Alexa) | "Lex" talks back |
| **Amazon Kendra** | Intelligent **enterprise search** across documents | Find answers in documents |
| **Amazon Personalize** | Real-time **recommendations** | "Customers also bought…" |
| **Amazon Fraud Detector** | Detect **online fraud** | Fraud |
| **Comprehend Medical / HealthScribe** | Health-specific NLP; generating **clinical notes** | Healthcare |

## The classic confusions

- **Polly speaks** (text → speech); **Transcribe listens** (speech → text).
- **Textract** reads **scanned documents**; **Comprehend** understands the **meaning** of text (sentiment).
- **Rekognition** is for **images and video**.
- **Lex** builds the chatbot; **Polly** gives it a voice.

## Worked scenarios

> Analyse customer reviews to see if they're positive or negative. → **Comprehend** (sentiment).

> Extract data from scanned invoices. → **Textract**.

> Turn recorded support calls into text. → **Transcribe**.

> Build a generative-AI assistant using Claude without managing infrastructure. → **Bedrock**.
